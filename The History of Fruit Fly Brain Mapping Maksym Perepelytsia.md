### The History of Fruit Fly Brain Mapping

Scientists began studying fruit flies in the early **1900s**, mainly to understand genetics. Later, researchers discovered that these tiny insects can learn, remember information, and make decisions.

With the development of powerful microscopes and computers, scientists started creating detailed maps of their brains.

In **2020**, researchers mapped around **25,000 neurons** in a large part of the fruit fly brain.

A major breakthrough happened in **2024**, when the FlyWire project created a complete brain map with approximately **140,000 neurons** and over **50 million connections**.

Interestingly, scientists used **artificial intelligence** to help reconstruct the brain and identify connections between neurons.

This discovery is an important step toward understanding how brains work, including the human brain.
