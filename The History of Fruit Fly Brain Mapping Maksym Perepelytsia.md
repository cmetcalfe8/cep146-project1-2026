### The History of Fruit Fly Brain Mapping

Scientists began studying fruit flies in the early *1900s*, mainly to understand genetics.

Later, new microscopes and computers helped researchers study their brains in more detail.

In *2020*, scientists mapped around *25,000 neurons* in a large part of the fruit fly brain.

In *2024*, the FlyWire project created a complete brain map with about *140,000 neurons* and over *50 million* connections.

This discovery helps scientists understand how brains work.
