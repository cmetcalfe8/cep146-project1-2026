### The History of Fruit Fly Brain Mapping

Scientists first started studying fruit flies in the early **1900s** to learn more about genetics. Over time, they found out that these tiny insects can actually learn, remember things, and make decisions.

As microscopes and computers improved, scientists were able to study their brains in much more detail.

In **2020**, researchers created a map of about **25,000 neurons** in a large part of the fruit fly brain.

Then, in **2024**, the FlyWire project made a huge breakthrough. Scientists created a map of the entire adult fruit fly brain, showing around **140,000 neurons** and more than **50 million connections**.

They also used **artificial intelligence** to help put all this information together.

This was a big step forward because it helps us understand how even tiny brains work and could help us learn more about the human brain in the future.
