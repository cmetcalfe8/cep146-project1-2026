# How the fly brain was reconstructed 

Scientists reconstructed the fruit fly brain by first, preserving the brain and cutting it into thousands of extremely thin slices. Each slice was photographed using an electron microscope, this produced very detailed images of the brain’s cells and structures. AI and computer software were used to trace individual neurons across the different images and combine them into a 3D model. Researchers then mapped the synapses, which are the connections between neurons, and manually checked the AI’s work for mistakes. The final result was a detailed wiring diagram of the fruit fly brain called a connectome.

The AI was especially important because the brain images contained millions of tiny structures that would take humans an extremely long time to trace by hand. The software helped identify and follow neurons from one brain slice to the next. Scientists then reviewed the results and corrected mistakes, allowing them to build a much more accurate map of how the neurons in the fly brain are connected.
