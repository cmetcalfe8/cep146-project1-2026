# How the fly brain was reconstructed 

Scientists reconstructed the fruit fly brain by first, preserving the brain and cutting it into thousands of extremely thin slices. Each slice was then photographed using an electron microscope, which produced very detailed images of the brain’s cells and structures. AI and computer-vision software were used to trace individual neurons across the different images and combine them into a 3D model. Researchers then mapped the synapses, which are the connections between neurons, and manually checked the AI’s work for mistakes. The final result was a detailed wiring diagram of the fruit fly brain called a connectome.
